Understand is the starting point in the [Unleash Learning Lab](https://learning.getunleash.io). The full course is hosted in the Learning Lab. This page gives you an overview before you begin.

Complete this course to earn the **FeatureOps Foundations** certificate.

## Who it's for

Everyone on the team, technical and non-technical. Understand gives the whole team a shared grasp of FeatureOps before anyone starts working with feature flags. No setup required.

## What you'll learn

- What FeatureOps is
- Why runtime control matters in modern development
- How FeatureOps changes the way you ship software
- Why FeatureOps is becoming essential to engineering

## Start the course

[Go to Understand in the Unleash Learning Lab](https://learning.getunleash.io/program/understand) to begin.
