Scale is the operations course in the [Unleash Learning Lab](/learning-lab). The full course is hosted in the Learning Lab. This page gives you an overview before you begin.

Complete this course to earn the **FeatureOps Administrator** certificate.

## Who it's for

Admins and platform teams responsible for running Unleash across the organization. This course turns Unleash from a tool one team uses into infrastructure your organization depends on.

## What you'll learn

- Administer your Unleash instance
- Operate at production scale across regions
- Integrate AI-native development practices

## Start the course

[Go to Scale in the Unleash Learning Lab](https://unleash-learning-lab.learnworlds.com/program/scale) to begin.

## Explore the other courses

- [Understand](/learning-lab/understand): For everyone on the team. The shared foundations of FeatureOps and runtime control.
- [Build](/learning-lab/build): For developers, architects, and engineers. Build flags, target users, and run experiments.
- [Champion](/learning-lab/champion): For people driving FeatureOps adoption across the organization.
