## Choose your learning path

The Learning Lab organizes content into four learning paths. Each path targets a different goal, so you can start with the foundations and go as deep as your role needs.

- **Understand**: Start here. The shared foundations everyone on the team needs, covering what FeatureOps is and why runtime control matters. Earns the **FeatureOps Foundations** certificate.
- **[Build](/learning-lab/build)**: The hands-on course for developers, architects, and engineers, from your first feature flag to automated, experiment-driven releases. Earns the **FeatureOps Practitioner** certificate.
- **[Scale](/learning-lab/scale)**: For admins and platform teams running Unleash for the whole organization, securely and reliably across regions. Earns the **FeatureOps Administrator** certificate.
- **[Champion](/learning-lab/champion)**: For people driving FeatureOps adoption, covering the internal case, governance, and demonstrating value to leadership. Earns the **FeatureOps Champion** certificate.

Ready to start? [Go to the Unleash Learning Lab](https://learning.getunleash.io) and pick your path.
