This page explains how Unleash plans are priced and billed: what you pay for, what is not metered, and where the limits are. For current plan prices and a full feature comparison, see [Unleash pricing](https://www.getunleash.io/pricing). For a feature-by-feature breakdown of the open-source and Enterprise editions, see [Unleash OSS and Unleash Enterprise comparison](/support/oss-comparison).

## What you pay for

Unleash Enterprise meters two things only: **seats** and, on Unleash Cloud, **API traffic above the included allowance**. [Enterprise add-ons](#enterprise-add-ons) and support tiers are priced separately and are not usage-metered.

### Seats

A seat is a user account in your Unleash instance. Seats are billed at a flat monthly rate per seat, so your cost depends on how many people have access, not how much they use Unleash.

A seat is a user in your Unleash instance. Every user listed in **Admin settings > User config > Users** uses one seat, regardless of their [root role](/concepts/rbac#predefined-roles) or how they were added: in the Unleash Admin UI, through single sign-on, or through [SCIM](/concepts/scim). This includes users who have not signed in yet.

[Service accounts](/concepts/service-accounts) do not count toward your licensed user count. Enterprise contracts can treat them differently.

When you remove a user, the seat stays counted for 30 days from the date you remove them. 

Seats are billed monthly, based on the number of users in your instance when the invoice is created. Each seat is charged at the full monthly price, with no partial-month pricing.

To see how many seats you currently use, go to **Admin settings > Users** in the Unleash Admin UI, which shows your current seat count and a graph of seats over time.

Seat limits depend on your hosting model:

- On Unleash Cloud, you can exceed your seat count. Unleash bills the additional seats monthly.
- On self-hosted instances, including pay-as-you-go, your [license key caps the seat count](/deploy/license-keys#seat-count). When you reach the limit, you cannot add users until you remove others or install a license with more seats. On pay-as-you-go, you add seats in [License Hub](https://license.getunleash.io).

Enterprise contracts can vary these terms, including the per-seat rate on an annual commitment. [Contact sales](https://www.getunleash.io/plans/enterprise) to discuss your contract.

### API traffic

On Unleash Cloud, the pay-as-you-go plan includes 53 million API requests per month. Requests beyond that are billed at \$5 per additional million. Enterprise traffic allowances are agreed in your contract.

API requests are generated by your SDKs communicating with Unleash, not by your end users interacting with your application. This distinction matters for forecasting:

- **Backend SDKs** fetch the flag configuration for their token's scope, cache it in memory, and evaluate flags locally. Evaluating a flag does not call Unleash. A service evaluating a flag a million times per second generates no additional API traffic.
- **Frontend SDKs** either call the [Frontend API](/concepts/front-end-api) or, more commonly at scale, an [Unleash Edge](/unleash-edge) instance, which absorbs client traffic and refreshes from the upstream Unleash server on its own schedule.
- **Refresh and metrics frequency** drives volume. Backend SDKs [refresh every 15 seconds and post metrics every 60 seconds by default](/sdks#default-refresh-and-metrics-intervals), and you can tune both per SDK.

These requests count toward your allowance:

- [Client API](/api/client-api-overview) requests from backend SDKs, and from [Unleash Edge](/unleash-edge) to the upstream Unleash server.
- [Frontend API](/concepts/front-end-api) requests from frontend SDKs.
- Metrics posts from SDKs and from Unleash Edge.
- [Admin API](/api/admin-api-overview) requests authenticated with an API token, such as those from scripts, CI pipelines, and integrations.

Working in the Admin UI from a signed-in browser session does not count. The allowance is measured per calendar month.

If you run [Unleash Edge](/unleash-edge), requests from your SDKs to Edge do not count toward your allowance, because they never reach the Unleash server. Edge's own requests to the upstream server do count, and each additional Edge instance adds its own upstream requests.

Your monthly request volume is therefore roughly the number of connected SDK instances multiplied by their refresh and metrics frequency.

<Accordion title="Estimate your monthly request volume">
Consider 200 backend SDK instances at the default intervals: a flag configuration request every 15 seconds and a metrics post every 60 seconds, or five requests per minute. That is about 216,000 requests per instance per month, and approximately 43.2 million across 200 instances, within the included 53 million allowance. Doubling the refresh interval to 30 seconds reduces the total by about 40%, because the metrics interval is unchanged.

Inverting the calculation, the included 53 million requests cover roughly 245 backend SDK instances at the default intervals. Unleash meters request volume, not connections: service connections are [not a billable unit](#what-is-not-metered), so adding instances or containers does not create a per-connection charge.
</Accordion>

If you exceed the allowance on pay-as-you-go, the additional requests are billed automatically at the overage rate. On an Enterprise contract, Unleash notifies you and the volume is addressed at renewal.

Sustained high traffic against the Unleash API is also subject to the [Fair Use Policy](https://www.getunleash.io/fair-use-policy). Unleash accommodates short spikes; for sustained volume, use [Unleash Edge](/unleash-edge).

## What is not metered

Unleash does **not** bill on any of the following, on any paid plan:

- Monthly active users (MAU) on client-side applications
- Flag evaluations
- Number of feature flags, within [resource limits](/concepts/resource-limits)
- Number of projects or environments, within [resource limits](/concepts/resource-limits)
- Service connections
- Number of SDKs or supported languages
- Environments used for development or testing

Because flag evaluation is not metered, there is no cost incentive to limit how many flags you create or how often you evaluate them. Putting every change behind a flag does not increase your bill.

## Data retention

<StickyTable>
| Data | Open Source | Pay-as-you-go | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Flag evaluation metrics](/concepts/insights#flag-evaluation-metrics) | 48 hours | 90 days | 90 days |
| [Impact metrics](/concepts/impact-metrics) | Not available | 6 months | 6 months |
| [Audit log](/concepts/events#event-log) | Basic | Advanced | Advanced |
| [Login history](/concepts/login-history) | Not available | Available | Available |
</StickyTable>

Impact metrics retention applies to internal metrics recorded through the SDK, which are available on Unleash Cloud only. If you self-host, use [external metrics](/concepts/impact-metrics#external-metrics), which are not stored in Unleash, so the limit does not apply to them.

The audit log is available at two levels:

- **Basic**: the full [event log](/concepts/events#event-log), which you can search, filter, and export as CSV or JSON.
- **Advanced**: everything in Basic, plus the [IP address](/concepts/events#ip-address) of the user who performed each action.

Audit log retention depends on your hosting model. On Unleash Cloud, Unleash retains audit logs for up to 2 years. On self-hosted instances, Unleash does not remove old events, so retention is determined by how you manage your database.

## Self-hosted Enterprise billing

Self-hosted deployments are billed on the same per-seat basis as Unleash Cloud. On pay-as-you-go, self-hosting has a 5-seat minimum; on an Enterprise contract, the minimum is agreed in your contract. You run the infrastructure; Unleash provides the Enterprise build and a license key. See [License keys](/deploy/license-keys) for how to install and rotate one.

Self-hosting removes the Unleash Cloud API traffic allowance, because traffic goes to your own servers. It does not remove the cost of running the service. You take on the infrastructure, upgrades, monitoring, and on-call coverage, and each additional Edge location adds to that.

In a large organization, this is usually a question of operational ownership rather than a significant new infrastructure line item. Unleash server instances and their PostgreSQL database are lightweight and typically run on Kubernetes capacity you already budget for, and a central platform engineering team can operate one installation for the whole company instead of each product team running its own. Because the per-seat price is the same either way, choosing between self-hosted and Unleash Cloud is a question of operational ownership and data residency, not of license cost.

## Trials

The pay-as-you-go plan includes a 14-day trial with no credit card required, on either [Unleash Cloud](https://www.getunleash.io/plans/enterprise-payg) or a [self-hosted instance](https://www.getunleash.io/plans/enterprise-payg-self-hosted). The trial provides the full Enterprise feature set.

When your trial ends, you'll get an email notification about the instance being scheduled for deletion. After a few more days, the instance and all its data will be deleted (no data retention). If you think you need more time to make a decision after the trial is over and want to avoid any data loss, reach out to our support team.

## Enterprise pricing

Unleash does not publish Enterprise contract prices. Enterprise agreements are quoted per customer because the price depends on variables that differ substantially between organizations:

- Number of seats
- Deployment model: Unleash Cloud, self-hosted, or hybrid
- API traffic volume and the number of Edge regions required
- Support tier and response-time requirements
- Add-ons such as private instances, access logs, and IP restrictions
- Data residency requirements

If you need a detailed quote, [contact sales](https://www.getunleash.io/plans/enterprise). To size a proposal accurately, have the following ready: your average daily frontend traffic, average session duration, and the number of application instances or Kubernetes pods that will connect to Unleash.

The [pay-as-you-go plan](https://www.getunleash.io/pricing) is priced publicly at \$75 per seat per month and requires no contract, so you can determine your cost for a self-service deployment without contacting us.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Does Unleash charge per monthly active user (MAU)?">
No. Unleash bills per seat, where a seat is a user of Unleash itself, not a user of your application. Client-side MAU is unlimited on all paid plans. Your bill does not change when your application's traffic grows.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Am I billed per flag evaluation?">
No. Backend SDKs fetch the flag configuration and evaluate flags locally in memory, so evaluations never reach Unleash and are never billed. Only SDK communication with the Unleash API counts toward your API traffic allowance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I exceed the included API requests?">
On the pay-as-you-go plan with Unleash Cloud, requests above the included 53 million per month are billed at \$5 per additional million. If you consistently exceed the allowance, [Unleash Edge](/unleash-edge) reduces upstream traffic by absorbing SDK requests, and Enterprise contracts can include a higher allowance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is Unleash free?">
Unleash Open Source is free and has no seat limit. It gives you the core feature set and is well suited to evaluation and small-scale deployments, with a limit of 1 project and 2 environments. It does not include SSO, SCIM, role-based access control beyond basic roles, change request approval workflows, or a support SLA. See the [full comparison](/support/oss-comparison).

Running Unleash Open Source is free to license but not free to operate. Budget for the infrastructure, upgrades, and on-call coverage you take on.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I self-host and still get Enterprise features?">
Yes. Enterprise is available as a self-hosted, hybrid, or Unleash Cloud deployment, at the same per-seat price. Self-hosting is common among customers with data residency or regulatory requirements, because flag evaluation and end-user context stay entirely within your infrastructure. See [hosting options](/deploy/hosting-options).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need a seat for everyone who needs to see a flag?">
Anyone with a user account in Unleash needs a seat, counted from when the account is created rather than from first sign-in. In practice, teams get the most value when developers have full visibility rather than routing changes through a small number of licensed users, and [change requests](/concepts/change-requests) provide four-eyes approval so broad access does not mean uncontrolled access.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I get an invoice instead of paying by card?">
Invoicing is available on Enterprise contracts. The pay-as-you-go plan is billed by credit card monthly. [Contact sales](https://www.getunleash.io/plans/enterprise) to move to an invoiced Enterprise agreement.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
